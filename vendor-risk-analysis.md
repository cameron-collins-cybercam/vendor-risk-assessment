# Vendor Risk Analysis

This document demonstrates how risks are evaluated after reviewing vendor security documentation.

---

## Vendor Name

Example Cloud Analytics Inc.

---

## Risk Category: Data Security

Risk Description:
Vendor stores customer analytics data within a shared cloud environment.

Risk Level:
Medium

Mitigation Strategy:
Ensure encryption at rest and strict access control policies.

---

## Risk Category: Access Control

Risk Description:
Vendor support staff may access customer data for troubleshooting.

Risk Level:
Medium

Mitigation Strategy:
Require least privilege access and logging of all administrative activity.

---

## Risk Category: Incident Response

Risk Description:
Vendor incident response procedures are not independently audited.

Risk Level:
Low

Mitigation Strategy:
Require SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification documentation.
