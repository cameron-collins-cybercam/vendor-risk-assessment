# Vendor Risk Rating Model

This model demonstrates how organizations classify vendor risk levels.

---

## Risk Categories

Low Risk
- No access to sensitive data
- No network integration
- Minimal operational impact

Medium Risk
- Limited access to internal systems
- Handles some sensitive data

High Risk
- Access to critical infrastructure
- Processes regulated or sensitive data
